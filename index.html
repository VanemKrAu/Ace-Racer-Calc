注意事项：Notes:
  • 新增计算模式：页面上方可在「先机汇能 / 团魂获能 / 辅助供能」之间切换。团魂获能：团魂、先机、汇能填在一处，能量来源在增益装置（单人竞速 / 组队竞速 / 巅峰赛，这三种模式没有反制和赋能，装上后两者自动锁定）与队友辅助之间二选一，结果并入首发与循环判定；辅助供能：选一辆辅助车，自动读取它给队友的辅助充能，算出队友实收多少。New calculation modes at the top: Xianji & Huineng / Team Soul Gain / Support Supply. Team Soul Gain puts Team Soul, Xianji and Huineng together and takes one energy source — a buff device (Solo / Team Race / Peak Match, which have no Counter or empowerments, so both lock while a device is on) or ally assist — and adds it to your first ult and loop. Support Supply reads a support car's ally charge and shows what an ally receives.
  • 选车后自动填充：大招阈值、基础双充（起手+氮气）、氮气时长、大招时长、起步额外充能、氮气自充能、大招自充能、每秒自充能、SP自充能、自定义触发自充能（触发次数需手填）。其他参数（芯片、赋能等）需手动调整。Auto-filled on car select: ult threshold, base dual charge, nitro duration, ult duration, start bonus, nitro self-charge, ult self-charge, per-sec charge, SP charge, custom-trigger self-charge (fill the trigger counts yourself). Other params (chips, empowers) need manual tuning.
  • 赛车选择器：点击顶部“选择赛车”标签，即可从完整赛车列表中挑选车辆，选中后点击“应用并返回”自动填充基础氮气充能值和大招时长。注意：自动填充仅覆盖基础参数，芯片、赋能等配置仍需手动微调。Car Selector: Tap "Select Car" tab to browse the full car list. Pick one, then click "Apply & Return" to auto-fill base nitro charge and ult duration. Note: chips, empowers still need manual tuning.
  • 关于社区方案与离线使用：核心计算功能与本地方案完全在本地运行，支持离线打开；若社区服务偶发网络波动，仅在线浏览、分享与点赞暂受影响，不影响本地计算与使用已保存的方案。Community Setups & Offline Use: The core calculator and your local setups work offline in your browser. If online service encounters temporary network issues, only online browsing, sharing, and voting are affected while local calculations continue working normally.
